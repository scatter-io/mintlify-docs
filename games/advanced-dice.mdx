### Overview

Advanced Dice extends classic Dice with richer bet shapes. Instead of betting on a single threshold, you choose one of three roll modes and configure either a single range or two disjoint ranges as your winning region.

### How to play

1. Choose a roll mode: Inside, Outside, or Between Two.
2. Drag the slider handles to set your bounds.
3. Place your bet.

A single random draw produces an integer between 0 and 10000. The display scale is 0.00 to 100.00. Whether you win is determined by checking the result against your chosen bounds.

### Rules and configuration

There are three roll modes:

- Inside: You set a single range `[lower, upper]`. You win if the result is strictly inside that range (`result > lower` and `result < upper`).
- Outside: You set a single range `[lower, upper]`. You win if the result is strictly outside that range (`result < lower` or `result > upper`). Each side of the range must contain at least one winning spot.
- Between Two: You set two non-overlapping ranges. You win if the result is strictly inside either range.

Bounds are strict at both ends in all modes. The minimum win range is 1, ensuring at least one winning spot. The maximum win range depends on the house edge: as edge increases, the maximum win range decreases.

The minimum win chance is 0.01%. The maximum win chance at a 1% edge is 98%, dropping with higher edges.

### Payouts

Win chance and payout multiplier are computed as follows:

```text
win_chance = (win_range / 10001) × 100
multiplier = (100 - edge) / win_chance
payout     = bet × multiplier
```

The `win_range` per mode:

- Inside: `upper - lower - 1`
- Outside: `lower + (10000 - upper)`
- Between Two: sum of the two inside ranges, computed the same way as Inside for each

If win chance is 0 (no winning spots possible), the configuration is rejected before the bet is placed.

### House edge

Advanced Dice is 1% edge.

### Limits

- Maximum bet: \$50,000 per wager, platform-wide cap.
- Minimum bet: configured on-chain per game.
- Multiplier cap: there is no explicit payout cap. The multiplier scales inversely with win chance up to the limit imposed by the edge and the minimum win chance.

### Fairness

Advanced Dice uses [VRF](/protocol/provable-fairness/verifiable-randomness) combined with seed commit-reveal for provable fairness. Each bet incorporates three inputs:

1. Server seed: a secret committed by Scatter ahead of time. The seed is rotated every 12 hours, and previous seeds are revealed so any past bet can be verified.
2. Client seed: a value managed by your client as a hash chain, advanced on each bet.
3. VRF output: an on-chain Verifiable Random Function output (MagicBlock Ephemeral VRF) that anyone can verify against its cryptographic proof.

The roll is derived deterministically from these inputs. The VRF guarantees the randomness was honestly generated. The seed commit-reveal guarantees the result was not manipulated by either party after the fact.

See [Provable Fairness](/protocol/provable-fairness/overview) for the broader model.

### Verification

Every Advanced Dice bet exposes the VRF request, proof, and consumed output on-chain, along with the client seed, server seed, roll type, and bounds.

- The Verify button on each bet entry re-derives the outcome from these inputs.
- The `/verify` page lets you paste a bet ID and reproduce the derivation end to end.
- All artifacts are queryable directly on Solana mainnet.
