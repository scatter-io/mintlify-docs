### Overview

Pick a target number of unique cards. The deck flips cards one at a time. If you reach your target without any duplicate appearing, you win at the multiplier for that target. If a duplicate appears first, the round ends with payout 0.

### How to play

1. Choose a target between 5 and 36 unique cards.
2. Place your bet.
3. Cards are drawn one at a time from a 52-card pool with replacement. The round ends when either:
   - Your target number of unique cards is reached (you win at the target's multiplier), or
   - A duplicate card appears (the round loses with payout 0).

### Rules and configuration

- Card pool: 52 cards.
- Sampling: with replacement. Each draw is an independent uniform sample from the 52-card pool.
- Target range: 5 to 36 unique cards.
- Round end: hitting your target (win) or any duplicate appearing (loss).

### House edge

The house edge is 1%.

### Limits

- Maximum bet: \$50,000 per wager, platform-wide cap.
- Minimum bet: configured on-chain per game.
- Maximum payout: configured on-chain. Bounded structurally by the largest multiplier (15,181,603.62× at target 36).

### Fairness

Blitz uses [VRF](/protocol/provable-fairness/verifiable-randomness) combined with seed commit-reveal for provable fairness. Each bet incorporates three inputs:

1. Server seed: a secret committed by Scatter ahead of time. The seed is rotated every 12 hours, and previous seeds are revealed so any past bet can be verified.
2. Client seed: a value managed by your client as a hash chain, advanced on each bet.
3. VRF output: an on-chain Verifiable Random Function output (MagicBlock Ephemeral VRF) that anyone can verify against its cryptographic proof.

The sequence of cards is derived deterministically from these inputs by repeatedly mapping segments of the VRF output to indices in the 52-card pool. Cards are drawn one at a time and checked against the cards drawn so far; the first duplicate or the target hit ends the round.

See [Provable Fairness](/protocol/provable-fairness/overview) for the broader model.

### Verification

Every Blitz round exposes the VRF request, proof, and consumed output on-chain, along with the client seed, server seed, target, and the full sequence of cards drawn.

- The Verify button on each round entry re-derives the outcome from these inputs.
- The `/verify` page lets you paste a round ID and reproduce the derivation card by card.
- All artifacts are queryable directly on Solana mainnet.
