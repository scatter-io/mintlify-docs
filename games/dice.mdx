### Overview

Classic provably-fair dice. Pick a target value and a direction, then roll. The roll resolves on a 0 to 9999 integer range (displayed as 0.00 to 99.99). You win when the roll lands on your chosen side of the target.

For richer bet shapes with multiple ranges and Inside / Outside / Between Two modes, see Advanced Dice.

### How to play

1. Choose a direction: Above or Below.
2. Set your target value. The slider runs from 0.05 to 99.95. You can also enter a target multiplier or win chance directly, and the UI back-solves the target.
3. Place your bet. A single integer in 0 to 9999 is drawn:
   * Above wins when the result is strictly greater than your target.
   * Below wins when the result is strictly less than your target.

### Rules and configuration

* Directions: Above or Below.
* Roll range: integer 0 to 9999 (10,000 buckets), displayed as 0.00 to 99.99.
* Target bounds: the UI restricts targets to 0.05 to 99.95. Multipliers and win chances outside the allowed range (0.01% minimum win chance, 98% maximum) are rejected.

### Payouts

Win chance and payout multiplier are computed as follows:

```
win_chance (Above) = (10000 − target) / 10000 × 100
win_chance (Below) = target / 10000 × 100
multiplier         = (100 − edge) / win_chance
payout             = bet × multiplier
```

A losing roll pays 0.

Worked example: with a target of 5000 (display 50.00) and direction Above, the win chance is 50% and the multiplier is `99 / 50 = 1.98×`.

### House edge

The house edge is 1%.

### Limits

* Maximum bet: $50,000 per wager, platform-wide cap.
* Minimum bet: configured on-chain per game.
* Maximum payout: configured on-chain. The program rejects bets where `bet × multiplier` would exceed this cap, even if the UI accepts the inputs.

### Fairness

Dice uses [VRF](/protocol/provable-fairness/verifiable-randomness) combined with seed commit-reveal for provable fairness. Each bet incorporates three inputs:

1. Server seed: a secret committed by Scatter ahead of time. The seed is rotated every 12 hours, and previous seeds are revealed so any past bet can be verified.
2. Client seed: a value managed by your client as a hash chain, advanced on each bet.
3. VRF output: an on-chain Verifiable Random Function output (MagicBlock Ephemeral VRF) that anyone can verify against its cryptographic proof.

The roll is derived deterministically from these inputs. The VRF guarantees the randomness was honestly generated. The seed commit-reveal guarantees the result was not manipulated after the fact.

See [Provable Fairness](/protocol/provable-fairness/overview) for the broader model.

### Verification

Every Dice bet exposes the VRF request, proof, and consumed output on-chain, along with the client seed, server seed, target, direction, and rolled number.

* The Verify button on each bet entry re-derives the outcome from these inputs.
* The `/verify` page lets you paste a bet ID and reproduce the derivation end to end.
* All artifacts are queryable directly on Solana mainnet.
