### Overview

Standard American roulette played with a zero and double-zero wheel. Place chips on any combination of inside and outside bets, then spin. One VRF-driven outcome resolves every chip on the board in a single transaction.

### How to play

1. Place chips on any combination of bet types (straight, split, street, corner, double street, basket, dozens, columns, parity, color, halves).
2. Spin the wheel.
3. The wheel produces a single result from `0`, `00`, or `1` through `36`. Every winning bet is paid at its multiplier; losing bets return 0. All chips resolve together in one transaction.

### Rules and configuration

* Wheel: American double-zero, 38 slots (`0`, `00`, and `1` through `36`).
* Multiple bets per spin: any combination of inside and outside bets, in any sizes.
* Outcome: a single result drawn from the 38 slots resolves the entire board.
* Zero and double-zero: both lose all outside bets (parity, color, half, column, dozen). Inside bets covering `0` or `00` (straight, split, street, basket, etc.) win normally.

### Payouts

Multipliers below are gross: the payout for a winning chip is `chip × multiplier`. A "2×" outside bet returns even money since you get back twice your stake.

| Bet | Numbers covered | Multiplier |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Straight | 1 | 36× |
| Split | 2 | 18× |
| Street | 3 | 12× |
| Corner | 4 | 9× |
| Basket (`0, 1, 2, 3, 00`) | 5 | 7× |
| Double Street (Line) | 6 | 6× |
| Column | 12 | 3× |
| Dozen | 12 | 3× |
| Parity (Even / Odd) | 18 | 2× |
| Color (Red / Black) | 18 | 2× |
| Half (Low 1 to 18 / High 19 to 36\) | 18 | 2× |

Multiple chips resolve independently. Every winning bet's `chip × multiplier` is summed into the total payout.

### House edge

The house edge is 5.3%, the standard American double-zero edge. Both the zero and double-zero losing every outside bet is the source of the edge.

### Limits

* Maximum bet: $50,000 per spin, platform-wide cap. The limit applies to the total chips placed.
* Minimum bet: configured on-chain per game.
* Maximum payout: configured on-chain. The client computes the best-case payout across all placed chips and rejects the bet if it would exceed the cap.
* Highest single multiplier: 36× (Straight up).

### Fairness

Roulette uses [VRF](/protocol/provable-fairness/verifiable-randomness) combined with seed commit-reveal for provable fairness. Each spin incorporates three inputs:

1. Server seed: a secret committed by Scatter ahead of time. The seed is rotated every 12 hours, and previous seeds are revealed so any past spin can be verified.
2. Client seed: a value managed by your client as a hash chain, advanced on each bet.
3. VRF output: an on-chain Verifiable Random Function output (MagicBlock Ephemeral VRF) that anyone can verify against its cryptographic proof.

The VRF output is reduced to a single result drawn from the 38 wheel slots (`0`, `00`, or `1` through `36`). Every placed chip is then settled deterministically against that result.

See [Provable Fairness](/protocol/provable-fairness/overview) for the broader model.

### Verification

Every Roulette spin exposes the VRF request, proof, and consumed output on-chain, along with the client seed, server seed, all chip placements, and the resolved number.

* The Verify button on each bet entry re-derives the outcome from these inputs.
* The `/verify` page lets you paste a spin ID and reproduce the derivation end to end.
* All artifacts are queryable directly on Solana mainnet.
