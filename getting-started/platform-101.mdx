Scatter Labs is a core contributor supporting the growth of the Scatter protocol.

The team has developed protocols on Solana since 2021. Members have previously were members of the founding team of [Shuffle.com](http://Shuffle.com) and contributors to the [SHFL Foundation](https://shfl.foundation/).
