Scatter is an onchain casino built on Solana. Every bet is placed, settled, and verifiable onchain provably fair from wager to payout.

The [Scatter Bankroll Vault](/protocol/scatter-bankroll-vault) is a community-owned vault that backs Cash bets on Scatter. Anyone can deposit Cash into the vault to take the other side of those bets. SHFL bets are backed separately by Scatter’s SHFL treasury.

[Games execute fully onchain](/protocol/game-engine) through audited smart contracts, and the protocol is non-custodial. Scatter funds stay in your wallet until a bet is placed, and every outcome is settled trustlessly. The system is composable, so anything built on Solana can plug into it.

Scatter is deliberately different from traditional gaming platforms. No deposit bonuses, no minimum wagers, no VIP hosts — just the fairest, most open place to play.
