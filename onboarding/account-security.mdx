---
title: "Account Security"
description: "Set up two-factor authentication, passkeys, and backup codes."
---

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds an extra verification step when signing in and approving withdrawals or tips. You can use an authenticator app, a passkey, or both.

## Enable an authenticator app

1. Open **Settings → Security**.
2. Select **Enable** beside **Authenticator app**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or enter the displayed secret key manually.
4. Enter the code generated by your authenticator app and select **Enable**.
5. Save the backup codes shown, then select **I've saved my codes**.

The method will show **Enabled** once setup is complete.

## Enable a passkey

1. Open **Settings → Security**.
2. Select **Enable** beside **Passkey**.
3. Follow your browser or device's prompts to create the passkey.

Passkeys require a compatible device and browser. You can keep your authenticator app enabled alongside your passkey.

## Keep your backup codes safe

Authenticator setup shows your backup codes once. Store them securely before closing the setup flow.

Each backup code can be used once to sign in or approve an action if you lose access to your authenticator app. Keep your secret key and backup codes private.

## Disable a method

Open **Settings → Security** and select **Disable** beside the method.

Removing an authenticator requires a current authentication code or an unused backup code. Removing a passkey requires verification through the app's verification prompt.
