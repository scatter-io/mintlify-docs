The Swap feature lets you convert between Cash and SHFL inside the Scatter app. It exists so you can easily switch which currency you bet in without leaving the platform.

### What you can swap

**Cash** (USDC on Solana) ↔ **SHFL**

Other directions and assets are not supported in the in-app swap. To convert from other assets, deposit through the [deposit modal](/onboarding/depositing) and the routing is handled for you.

### How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Swap">
    Open Swap in the Scatter app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose direction">
    Choose Cash → SHFL or SHFL → Cash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount">
    Enter the amount to swap. The expected output is shown before you confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm transaction">
    Confirm the transaction from your embedded wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The swap is executed against the SHFL/USDC pool on Raydium. The transaction is non-custodial: tokens transfer between your wallet and the pool directly, with no intermediate Scatter-controlled balance.

### Liquidity and slippage

Large swaps can move the pool price meaningfully, leading to higher price impact and worse execution than the quoted rate suggests.

<Warning>
  If the pool price moves beyond your slippage tolerance between quote and execution, the transaction may revert.
</Warning>

Before swapping a large amount:

* Check the price impact shown in the swap UI. A large impact indicates thin liquidity at your size.
* Consider splitting the swap into smaller transactions across time.
* Set a tighter slippage tolerance to prevent execution at unexpectedly bad prices.

### Fees

* Pool fees: The Raydium fee for the SHFL/USDC pool is included in the quoted output. The quote reflects the actual amount you will receive at the current pool price.
* Network fees: Standard Solana transaction fees apply.

Scatter does not charge an additional swap fee. The full route cost is the Raydium fee plus network costs.

### Limits

There is no minimum swap amount enforced by Scatter, though very small swaps may not be cost-effective due to network fees.

The maximum swap amount is bounded by available liquidity in the underlying pool.
