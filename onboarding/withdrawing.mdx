title: "Withdraw"
description: "Learn how withdrawals work on Scatter."
---

Scatter is non-custodial. Your Cash and SHFL balances sit in your embedded wallet, so withdrawing simply moves funds out of your wallet to a destination of your choice.

### Before you withdraw

You need a positive Cash or SHFL balance in your Scatter wallet.

### What you can withdraw

Scatter holds two balance types on Solana: Cash (USDC on Solana) and SHFL. You can withdraw either directly to a Solana address, or convert and send to a different chain through the withdraw modal.

<Note>
  The exact supported destinations, routing path, network fee, and expected output are shown in the withdraw modal before you confirm.
</Note>

Supported destinations, tokens, and minimum withdrawal amounts can change. Open **Withdraw** and check the available options and requirements for your chosen destination. Deposit and withdrawal support may differ.

### Withdraw timing

Withdrawal timing depends on protocol processing, the selected route, and destination-chain confirmations.

Some requests require additional processing or review. Network congestion, routing-provider delays, or a pause in withdrawals can also increase the time needed to complete a withdrawal.

Any estimated completion time is indicative, not guaranteed. Funds become available at your destination after the transfer completes and the destination chain confirms it.

### Fees and refunds

- Withdrawals may include a protocol withdrawal fee in addition to network fees. Any applicable fees are deducted from the withdrawal amount.
* Failed swaps are refunded automatically to your Scatter wallet. Refund timing varies by chain.
