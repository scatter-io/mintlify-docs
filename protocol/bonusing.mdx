---
title: "Bonusing"
description: "Learn how Scatter rakeback, reloads, and level-up rewards work."
---

Scatter operates a transparent, mechanical bonusing system. All rewards are calculated automatically from on-chain activity and credited without manual review, wagering requirements, or expiry. There are no sticky bonuses, opaque promotions, or comp negotiations.

The system has four components: rakeback, reload, level-up, and [referral](/protocol/referrals).

## Rakeback

Rakeback returns a portion of every bet placed back to the player as Cash. The amount is calculated from total wager volume and is credited as soon as the rakeback balance >0.01 USD

Key properties:

* Calculated from gross wager volume, not from net losses
* Paid in Cash or SHFL with no wagering requirement
* No expiry on credited rakeback
* Rate scales with the player's current level (see Level-up)

## Reload

Reload pays a decaying share of a player's accrued edge over 7 days. The schedule starts at 15% on day 0 and decays to 1% on day 6, totaling 40% of accrued edge across the week. Reloads do not expire.

| Day | % of Accrued Edge | Example ($1,000 accrued edge) |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 0 | 15% | $150 |
| 1 | 10% | $100 |
| 2 | 5% | $50 |
| 3 | 4% | $40 |
| 4 | 3% | $30 |
| 5 | 2% | $20 |
| 6 | 1% | $10 |
| Total | 40% | $400 |

Accrued edge is the theoretical gross gaming revenue (theo GGR) generated by the player's wagers during the period.

## Level-up

Level-up is Scatter's player progression system. Every bet contributes to lifetime wager volume, which determines the player's current level. Crossing each level threshold pays out a bonus equal to 10% of the edge generated on the wager volume since the previous level.

| Level | Cumulative Wager Threshold | Bonus |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| L1 | $100 | $0.10 |
| L2 | $5,000 | $4.90 |
| L3 | $10,000 | $5.00 |
| L4 | $20,000 | $10.00 |
| L5 | $50,000 | $30.00 |
| L6 | $100,000 | $50.00 |
| L7 | $500,000 | $400.00 |
| L8 | $5,000,000 | $4,500.00 |
| L9 | $10,000,000 | $5,000.00 |
| L10 | $20,000,000 | +10% rakeback boost (no Cash bonus) |

L10 grants a permanent +10% boost to the player's rakeback rate in lieu of a one-time bonus.

Levels are deterministic and based purely on wager volume. There is no application process, no host relationship, and no discretionary upgrades.
