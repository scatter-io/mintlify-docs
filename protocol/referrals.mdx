Scatter's referral program pays a share of the edge generated by players you refer. Rates are tiered, transparent, and credited automatically. There are no application processes or approval gates.

### How it works

Every Scatter user has a unique referral link. When a new player signs up using your link, they are permanently attributed to you. From that point forward, you earn a share of the edge generated by their wagers across every game on the platform.

Referral attribution is one-time and lifetime: once a player is linked to your account, no other referrer can claim them.

### Earnings

Your rate scales with the cumulative wager generated by your referred players. Rates are incremental, meaning each tier's rate applies only to the marginal volume within that tier, not retroactively to prior volume. An example of earnings of a 1% edge game

| Cumulative Referred Wager | Rate | Example Earnings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1,000,000 | 10% | \$1,000 |
| 1,000,000 to 5,000,000 | 11% | \$5,500 |
| 5,000,000 to 10,000,000 | 12% | \$12,000 |
| 10,000,00050,000,000 | 13% | \$50,000 |
| \$50,000,000+ | 15% | \$75,000 |

Rates apply to the edge generated by referred players, not to the gross wager amount.

### Payouts

Referral earnings are calculated continuously from referred-player activity and credited as Cash on a fixed schedule. Earnings do not expire.

### Verification

All referral attribution and earnings are derived from on-chain activity. You can reconcile your referral earnings against your referred players' bet history at any time.
