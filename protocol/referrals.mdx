Scatter's referral program pays a share of the edge generated by players you refer. Rates are tiered and transparent. There are no application processes or approval gates.

### How it works

Every Scatter user has a unique referral link. When a new player signs up using your link, they are permanently attributed to you. From that point forward, you earn a share of the edge generated by their wagers across every game on the platform.

Referral attribution is one-time and lifetime: once a player is linked to your account, no other referrer can claim them.

### Earnings

Your commission rate is blended across tiers based on your referred players’ lifetime eligible wager volume, measured in USD.

| Referred wager band | Tier rate |
| --- | --- |
| First 1,000,000 USD | 10% |
| Next 4,000,000 USD | 11% |
| Next 5,000,000 USD | 12% |
| Next 40,000,000 USD | 13% |
| Above 50,000,000 USD | 15% |

Each band contributes proportionally to your blended rate. That rate applies to the total house edge generated by your referred players in each currency. Each claim calculates your total commission earned to date and subtracts amounts already credited.

For example, 2,000,000 USD in eligible referred wagering gives a blended rate of **10.5%**: half at 10% and half at 11%. If those Cash bets generated 20,000 USD of house edge, your total commission would be **2,100 USD in Cash**, before subtracting previously credited earnings.

### Payouts

Referral earnings accumulate from eligible play by the players you refer. Earnings from Cash bets are paid in Cash, and earnings from SHFL bets are paid in SHFL.

Claim your available referral earnings to add them to your Scatter balance. Earnings do not expire.

### Verification

All referral attribution and earnings are derived from on-chain activity. You can reconcile your referral earnings against your referred players' bet history at any time.
