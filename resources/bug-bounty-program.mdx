---
title: "Vulnerability Reporting"
description: "Learn how to report security vulnerabilities to Scatter."
---

If you discover a potential security vulnerability in Scatter, report it privately to [bugs@scatter.io](mailto:bugs@scatter.io).

### What to include

- The affected page, feature, or contract address, including the network.
- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce it.
- Relevant transaction IDs, screenshots, or a minimal proof of concept.

Do not include passwords, private keys, seed phrases, or other sensitive credentials.

### Responsible reporting

Please keep vulnerability details private while we investigate. Avoid testing that could affect other users, expose their data, or disrupt the platform.

This reporting channel does not promise monetary rewards.
