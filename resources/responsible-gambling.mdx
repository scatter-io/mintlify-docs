Manage your gambling limits and breaks from **Settings**. Choose **Gambling limits** to manage your spending, or **Self-exclusion** to take time away from betting.

## Gambling limits

You can set two types of limit:

- **Wager limit:** Tracks the total amount wagered, regardless of whether bets win or lose.
- **Loss limit:** Tracks your net losses: stakes minus payouts, with a minimum of zero.

Limits are measured in USD across Cash and SHFL bets together. They are not separate limits for each currency. Progress updates as bets settle.

### Set a limit

1. Open **Settings → Gambling limits**.
2. Select **Set Gambling Limit**.
3. Choose the limit type, enter an amount in USD, and select **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
4. Select **Add Limit**.

A new limit takes effect once saved. Daily limits reset every 24 hours, weekly limits every 7 days, and monthly limits every 30 days, starting from when you created the limit. For example, a daily limit created at 2pm resets at 2pm the following day.

You can view each limit's amount, reset schedule, and progress in the limits table.

### Change or remove a limit

To lower a limit, select **Set Gambling Limit**, choose the same type and period, and enter a lower amount. The lower limit applies immediately and keeps your existing progress and reset schedule.

To remove a limit, select **Remove** beside it and confirm. The limit stays active during a **24-hour cooling-off period**. To increase a limit, remove the existing one, wait for that period to finish, then set the new amount.

### How limits work

The app checks your recorded usage before betting. A wager limit checks whether the next stake would exceed your remaining allowance. A loss limit checks whether your recorded net losses have already reached the limit; an individual bet can take your losses above it.

**Gambling limits are currently advisory controls in the Scatter app, not a guaranteed spending cap.** Usage updates can lag behind play, and limits may not block betting if they cannot be loaded. Use self-exclusion if you want to block betting on your account.

## Take a break and self-exclude

Open **Settings → Self-exclusion** and select **Continue**. The initial option is a **24-hour break**. Review the confirmation details, complete the acknowledgements, and select **Confirm self-exclusion**. Your break begins when the confirmation transaction succeeds.

During a break, betting is blocked on your account. You can still access the platform, withdraw your balance, and claim rewards you have already earned.

### Extend your break

While an eligible break is active, select **Modify** to see the available extensions. The duration menu includes **1 day**, **1 week**, **1 month (30 days)**, and **6 months (180 days)**. Only options that maintain or extend your current end time can be selected.

You cannot shorten or cancel a break yourself. A six-month self-exclusion cannot be modified through the app while it is active. Timed restrictions expire automatically at the end of the selected period.

### Permanent self-exclusion

For permanent self-exclusion, contact **Customer Support**. Selecting **Permanent self-exclusion** in the duration menu opens the support route; selecting the option alone does not activate it.

You can also email [support@scatter.com](mailto:support@scatter.com). Once applied, permanent self-exclusion has no automatic expiry.
